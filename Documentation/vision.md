**EverRealm — Vision**  
*Intent, not a roadmap. This page describes what EverRealm is meant to become so that today's decisions can be checked against it. EverRealm is a large, long-term project: everything below is where the game is going. Milestones decide the order and shape of the work, not whether it happens. This page holds no schedule, order, or architecture — the game is built through small playable milestones.*  
**The fantasy**  
EverRealm is a procedural fantasy sandbox RPG built around **exploration, combat, world simulation, and player freedom**. It draws on three games:  
- **Minecraft** — a procedural world that feels explorable and mutable.  
- **Cube World** — adventurous fantasy exploration and satisfying action combat.  
- **Mount & Blade: Bannerlord** — settlements, factions, armies, politics, recruitment, and a world that feels like it exists beyond the player.  
**Principles**  
- **The player is not special.** The player is an ordinary world agent whose intentions come from input rather than AI. They follow the same rules and capabilities as other human/humanoid characters; other species can differ.  
- **Death is permanent.** Eventually you continue as a child or heir (succession) rather than respawning.  
- **Combat is simple with depth.** Depth comes from timing, spacing, positioning, and weapon reach — not from a large moveset. How a character dodges depends on how much they carry.  
- **The world lives without you.** Agents, factions, and events should feel like they would carry on whether or not the player is watching.  
- **Multiplayer someday.** Not built yet, but gameplay shouldn't depend on global, single-player-only shortcuts.  
**Long-term**  
Procedural terrain, towns and cities, kingdoms and factions, NPCs with their own lives, combat, dungeons, armies, politics, progression, and other sandbox systems.  
**How it gets built**  
Small playable milestones, each proving one part of the fantasy before expanding. Prototypes over predetermined architecture; the simplest design that correctly supports what is being built now.
